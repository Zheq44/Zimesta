07 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

Here are a few things you may want to know before getting started.

How do I request a service or quotation?+

Use the contact form or WhatsApp button on this website. Tell us which service you need and a little about your project. We will discuss the requirements and confirm a quotation where the service is available.

Does ZIMESTA work with businesses and individuals?+

Yes. ZIMESTA aims to support individuals, entrepreneurs, small businesses, schools and organisations in Zambia.

Can I request a website or a custom design?+

Yes. Send us your idea, goals and any reference materials. We can discuss the scope, features, timing and cost before work begins.

Do you keep computer equipment in stock?+

Equipment enquiries are handled on request. Product availability, condition, supplier details, price and any warranty must be confirmed before an order is agreed.

Are all services available immediately?+

Availability depends on the service and project requirements. Contact us first so we can confirm whether we can take on the work or recommend an appropriate specialist.
